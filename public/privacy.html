Privacy Policy

Liza Board · last updated 25 November 2026

What we keep

  • Your Google account basics: name, email address and profile photo, to sign you in. We never see your Google password.
  • Your boards: what you draw and write, saved to your account so you can open it on any device.
  • A short activity history: the names of things you did and when ("made a mind map: water cycle", "asked: what is gravity"), shown to you under My history and to the site administrator. Not your drawings.
  • Classes: if you join a class, a small picture of your current board goes to that class's teacher while you are in it.
  • Usage counts: how many AI requests you made each day, to keep the service fair.
  • One cookie that keeps you signed in. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Who helps us run it

To read your writing and answer your questions, the part of the board you ask about (as a picture or as text) is sent to AI services that process it for us: OpenRouter and the model providers it routes to, Groq (speech), Cartesia (Liza's voice) and fal.ai (pictures). We use them only to answer you. The site runs on Amazon Web Services and sign-in is by Google.

Children

Many people using the board are school students. When the board is used through a school, the school is responsible for getting a parent's or guardian's consent for students under 18, as required by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We never sell students' data, never show advertising, and never use it to build profiles for anyone else.

How long we keep it

Your boards and history stay until you delete them or your account. Daily backups are kept for 14 days and then overwritten.

Your choices

  • See your history any time under My history.
  • Delete any board from My boards.
  • Delete your whole account and everything above with Delete my account in your profile menu. It takes effect at once (backups clear within 14 days).
  • For any other request or complaint, contact the administrator who gave you access, or the school that runs your account.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so on the board before it takes effect.
